How it works

From upload to release, in three stages.

STAGE 01

Ingest and analyze

Drop in scanned files or native PDFs and processing starts on its own.

  • AI-powered OCR indexes every page, in real time or on a background queue
  • Personal data flagged automatically across eight categories, including SSNs, emails, phone numbers and names
  • Metadata captured on the way in, and editable afterwards
STAGE 02

Search and redact

Find what matters, and remove what shouldn't leave.

  • Vector rendering that stays sharp from 50% to 300% zoom
  • Full-text search across the repository, limited to what each user is allowed to see
  • Burn AI-flagged fields in one click, or draw your own boxes
  • Rotate, reorder, delete, append and merge pages inside the viewer
STAGE 03

Govern and release

Send documents out without losing track of them.

  • Download, print, or email through time-limited links, never permanent URLs
  • Role-based permissions enforced on the server and tied to your identity provider
  • Every view, edit, redaction and export written to an immutable audit trail
  • Expungement destroys the file and everything derived from it, while the audit record survives
Capabilities

An assistant that knows the document in front of it.

Ask Docket

Ask questions about a document and get answers that cite the page they came from.

Redaction by instruction

Ask in plain language, for example to redact every account number, and confirm before anything is burned.

PII explainer

See why something was flagged and how confident the detection was before you decide.

Permanent redaction

Redactions are burned into the file itself, not laid over it as an overlay someone can remove.

Expungement

Destroy a file and every copy derived from it, while keeping the record that it happened.

Embeddable

Put Docket inside your own web or desktop application through a postMessage contract.
