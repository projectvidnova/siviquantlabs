How it works

From property record to filed certificate.

STAGE 01

Classify and schedule

Every property, on the schedule the code sets.

  • One database of addresses, building types, occupancy classifications and contacts, searchable and filterable
  • Properties classified as life hazard or non-life hazard, with construction-date rules (before and after 1979) applied automatically
  • Inspection intervals generated from the code: up to four a year for schools, at most five years for non-life-hazard properties
  • Checklist templates aligned to the fire code, managed by admins
STAGE 02

Inspect and flag

Field work captured once, at the property.

  • Photos and checklists captured offline and synced automatically when the device reconnects
  • AI reviews field photos against the active checklist and drafts findings with suggested code citations
  • Nothing becomes a violation until the inspector confirms it
  • A code lookup tool to check any citation
STAGE 03

Certify and file

Every violation followed through to the end.

  • Each violation tracked in one record, from citation through correction and re-inspection
  • Inspection reports and narratives drafted from structured findings
  • Notifications for upcoming inspections, overdue tasks and critical findings
  • Certificates filed with the full audit history attached
